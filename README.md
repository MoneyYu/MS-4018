## Mind Map
```markmap
# Draft, analyze, and present with Microsoft 365 Copilot (MS-4018-A)
## M01 - Get ready to work with Microsoft Copilot
### Choose where to work
- [Microsoft Copilot](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/modules/get-ready-work-microsoft-365-copilot/) uses prompts, app context, organizational data, and sometimes the web
- Start with Chat to explore, an app to create, or an agent for multi-step work
### Ground and protect information
- Work IQ links emails, files, meetings, and chats within existing permissions
- [Enterprise data protection](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/enterprise-data-protection) and sensitivity labels protect organizational content
### Prompt with intention
- Context, goal, source, expectations; refine and verify the result
## M02 - Unlock productivity and unleash creativity with AI powered chat
### Find the right context
- [Copilot Chat](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-copilot/get-started-with-microsoft-365-copilot-chat): work-grounded vs web-grounded responses
- Search finds information; Chat synthesizes it into an answer
### Work iteratively
- Reference files, choose response depth, and follow up on incomplete answers
- Prompt Coach helps refine prompts; [Copilot Pages](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-copilot/get-started-with-microsoft-365-copilot-pages) preserves shareable output
## M03 - Build effective presentations with AI
### Plan the story
- Idea Coach: audience, purpose, narrative, and outline
- [Create a presentation](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/powerpoint/copilot/create-a-new-presentation-with-copilot-in-powerpoint) using a clear brief and grounded source files
### Refine and deliver
- Improve structure, slides, visuals, and speaker notes
- Review citations, brand consistency, accessibility, and translated slides
## M04 - Draft impactful documents using AI
### Draft from context
- [Draft with Copilot in Word](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/word/copilot/draft-and-add-content-with-copilot-in-word) using accessible files, emails, and meetings
- Provide audience, tone, structure, and source constraints
### Revise with intent
- Rewrite sections; summarize and compare evidence before accepting changes
- Writing Coach gives focused feedback; humans review the final document
## M05 - Make your meetings more productive with AI
### Before and during the meeting
- Prepare agendas and catch up on chats, files, and earlier decisions
- [Copilot in Teams](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/teams/copilot/catch-up-on-meetings-with-microsoft-365-copilot-in-teams) answers in-meeting questions
### Capture outcomes
- [Facilitator](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/teams/copilot/facilitator-in-microsoft-teams-meetings) structures agendas, notes, and actions
- Intelligent Recap and audio recap help review decisions and follow-ups
## M06 - Uncover new data insights with AI
### Prepare trustworthy data
- [Copilot in Excel](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/excel/copilot/get-started-with-copilot-in-excel) works best with structured, accessible workbooks
- Use Chat, Plan, or Edit mode depending on whether changes are permitted
### Analyze and communicate
- Explore formulas, PivotTables, forecasts, and Python-driven analysis
- Analyst explores across files; verify calculations and assumptions
## M07 - From inbox to impact: Improve your email workflows with AI
### Triage the inbox
- Summarize threads and attachments, prioritize messages, identify actions
### Draft and follow through
- [Draft with Copilot in Outlook](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/outlook/copilot-pages/draft-an-email-message-with-copilot-in-outlook), apply instructions, and refine with coaching
- Turn discussions into meetings and review recipients and facts before sending
```
